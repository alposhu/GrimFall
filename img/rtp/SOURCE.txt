RPG Maker MZ material
=====================

These eight files are derived from the Runtime Package that ships with RPG
Maker MZ, from the project owner's own licensed installation. They are not
loose copies of the RTP: `tools/assets/build-rtp-art.py` cuts out only the
slices the game actually draws, tones the ground and props down for a market
lit by braziers rather than by noon, and packs the result into atlases. Re-run
that script to rebuild them.

WHAT IS HERE
  ground.png    240x48   Five 48px paving stones (Outside_A5), darkened.
  props.png     1152x576 32 market props on a 144px grid, bottom-anchored in
                         their cell so a tall prop just occupies more of it.
                         Sources: Outside_B.
  signs.png     432x48   Nine hanging trade signs (Outside_B rows 8-9).
  banners.png   192x96   Four hanging cloth banners (Outside_C).
  folk.png      864x576  The market's cast: fifteen ordinary townsfolk then
                         the three merchants, as 3x4-cell blocks six to a row.
                         Cut from People1-4, but NOT all of them - People3 is
                         a royal court and the other sheets carry more nobles,
                         a bride and a priestess. Somebody haggling over
                         turnips in ermine is absurd, so only people who look
                         like they buy their own food are included.
  actors.png    576x384  Actor2 - the eight adventurers. Five of the seven
                         heroes wear one; Ada and Leon do not.
  actor_faces.png 576x288  Actor2 portraits, in the same order.
  balloons.png  384x480  Balloon - ten expressions, eight frames each.
  items.png     192x96   One icon per shop good, cut from IconSet and keyed
                         by the good's own id - so a Whetstone is a smith's
                         hammer, a Commission is a sword, a Clockwork Spring
                         is an hourglass and a Wax Effigy is a doll. No two
                         goods share a picture.

HOW THE GAME USES THEM
  `src/art/rtp.js` decodes these during boot and hands out slices. Every one of
  them has a code-drawn equivalent that predates it - `src/art/market.js`,
  `folk.js`, `balloons.js`, `items.js` - and that is what draws if a file is
  missing, blocked, or still loading. The market never waits on artwork.

  The rest of the game stays code-drawn. These are the market's art, not the
  game's: the field, the enemies, the bosses and the heroes are all still
  generated from pixel maps at boot.
