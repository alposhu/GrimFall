CHANGES MADE (required by the licence)
  The originals are 96 kHz / 24-bit stereo WAV. For use on the web, each clip
  here has been:
    - downmixed to mono
    - resampled to 32 kHz
    - converted to 16-bit PCM
    - trimmed of leading and trailing silence
    - length-capped with a short fade for the sounds that fire most often
    - peak-normalised
  No clip has been re-recorded, pitch-shifted on disk, or altered in content.
  The game does apply a small random playback-rate variation at runtime so a
  sound heard several times a second does not sound pasted.
  Only 66 of the pack's 2100 clips are included.
