CHANGES MADE (required by the licence)
  The originals are 32-bit float stereo WAV at 44.1 kHz. For use on the web,
  each clip here has been:
    - downmixed to mono
    - resampled to 22.05 kHz
    - converted to 16-bit PCM
    - trimmed of leading and trailing silence
    - peak-normalised
  No clip has been re-recorded, pitch-shifted, or altered in content.
  Only a curated subset of the pack's 1090 clips is included.
