How to Play

Move

WASD or the arrow keys. On a phone, drag anywhere on the lower half of the screen. A gamepad stick works too.

Attack

You never attack manually. Your weapons fire on their own — positioning is the combat. Keep moving, keep the horde in front of you.

Grow

Green shards level you up. Each level offers three upgrades: a new weapon, a stronger one, or a passive. Six weapons and six passives is the cap, so choose a build and commit. Most passives cap at five levels; health and armour go to twenty-five.

An enemy sometimes drops a lodestone. Pick it up and for seven seconds every shard, coin, heart and meal on the field comes to you at speed — walk into a boss's leavings and let it clear the ground for you.

Evolve

Take a weapon to level 8 while its paired passive is at level 3 or more, and an evolution appears in your next level-up. Evolutions are dramatically stronger.

Survive

Champions arrive every 85 seconds, and a boss lands every four minutes — each with its own entrance. At twenty minutes the sky opens for Parduin, the Drake God. Kill him and the run is won.

A dead boss takes the whole field with him and tears a portal open a short walk away — follow the arrow. It waits, so sweep up what he dropped first. Stepping through asks where you want to come out: the Long Market, or straight on into open country.

Boss Arena

Want to see a boss without playing to it? Boss Arena on the main menu drops you straight into any fight, with a loadout matched to where you would normally meet it. Practice only — no gold is banked.

Eat

The horde sometimes drops a meal — etli ekmek, dumplings, seven kinds of sushi, and rarer things like a slab of lasagna, a heaven persimmon or a pinch of Delice salt. Food is the only thing that restores health, and none of it is ever wasted: whatever it cannot heal because you are already full turns into experience instead. Champions and bosses always leave some behind.

The Long Market

Kill a boss and the traders come back out. Walk the square, talk to Oswin, Marta and the Coinweigher, and spend your run gold on weapons, draughts and charms. Gold spent here never reaches the Sanctuary — that is the trade: this run, or the next one.

Save & Resume

Walking into the market writes an autosave, so a long run survives a closed tab. You can also save to one of three slots from the pause menu, and pick any of them back up from the title screen.

Carry it out

Gold survives death. Spend it in the Sanctuary on permanent upgrades and new heroes, then go again.
