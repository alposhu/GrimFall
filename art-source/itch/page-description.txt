------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Twenty minutes. One hero. Everything else wants you dead.

You never press attack. You move — your weapons fire themselves, and the only
question is whether your build outgrows the horde before it closes over you.
Survive the long hour, bring down Parduin the Drake God, and carry your gold
into the next run.

Plays in your browser. Nothing to install.


WHAT'S IN IT

• 14 weapons, each with an evolution — auto-aiming bolts, a returning glaive,
  chain lightning, a two-sided lash, and Mjolnir, which you throw and then
  watch hunt the crowd while it throws lightning of its own.
• 5 bosses, each with an animated entrance, ending with a dragon.
• The Long Market — kill a boss, step through the portal it leaves, and spend
  your run gold on weapons, draughts and charms. Gold spent there never reaches
  the Sanctuary: that's the trade, this run or the next one.
• 7 heroes, unlocked with gold that survives death.
• Four save slots, plus save-to-file so your progress isn't trapped in one
  browser on one device.
• Full touch controls. It plays properly on a phone.


SOURCE CODE

Grimfall is open source, MIT licensed:
https://github.com/alposhu/GrimFall

Almost none of the art is an image file — the sprites are pixel maps rasterised
when the game boots, and the world is a pure function of position, so the map
costs no memory and never repeats a seam.
