Budgie familiars
================

The four birds that fly with you are drawn from a third-party pixel pack.

SOURCE
  "Budgie / Parakeet Birds Pixel Asset Pack" by Pop Shop Packs
  https://pop-shop-packs.itch.io/budgie-parakeet-birds-pixel-asset-pack
  Free / name-your-own-price. Supplied to the project as
  `Budgies_Spritesheets.zip`.

LICENCE (as published on that page)
  - Usable in any project, personal or commercial.
  - May be modified to suit.
  - Credit is not required, but is appreciated, with a link to the author's
    itch.io profile.
  - MUST NOT be resold or redistributed AS A GAME ASSET. Use has to be as part
    of a project.
  - MUST NOT be turned into an NFT.
  - MUST NOT be fed to generative A.I. software.

  Everything this game does with them is squarely inside those terms: they are
  modified, they are part of a project, and they are credited on the game's own
  credits screen and in README.md even though the licence does not demand it.
  That matches how this project treats the music, which also does not require
  attribution and gets it anyway.

WHY THE ORIGINAL PNGs ARE NOT IN THIS FOLDER
  They were here briefly and were removed on purpose. This repository is
  public, so four unmodified sheets sitting in a folder called `art-source`
  are downloadable as loose files - which is nearer to redistributing the pack
  than to using it in a project, and the licence draws exactly that line. The
  pack is free and permanently available at the link above, so nothing is lost
  by pointing at it instead of copying it.

  The NFT and generative-A.I. clauses are also worth keeping in view: do not
  re-add the raw sheets, and do not feed them to an image model.

WHAT THE GAME SHIPS INSTEAD
  `src/art/familiars.js` holds one flight cycle as a pixel map plus four
  palettes. The four sheets turned out to be palette swaps of a single drawing
  - identical alpha masks, identical pixel counts - so four copies of the same
  geometry would have been four times the bytes for no extra art. The
  extraction asserted the shapes matched pixel-for-pixel before the maps were
  written.

  Only the first column of the sheet is used: a four-frame flight cycle at
  16x15, east facing, mirrored for west. The perched and standing poses in
  columns 1 and 2, and the `branches` and `Bungie Case` sheets, are unused.

TO REBUILD FROM THE PACK
  Download it from the link above, then take column 0 (four 16x16 cells) of
  `Blue Budgie.png` as the geometry and read each variant's eight colours off
  the same pixel positions in the other three sheets. That is exactly how the
  maps and palettes in `src/art/familiars.js` were produced.
