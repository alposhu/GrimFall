Budgie spritesheets
===================

The four bird sheets these came from, kept here as the source of the familiar
art. They are NOT loaded by the game and are not in the shipped build:
`tools/build.mjs` ships an allowlist of folders and art-source is not on it.

WHAT THE GAME ACTUALLY USES
  `src/art/familiars.js` holds one flight cycle as a pixel map plus four
  palettes. The four sheets turned out to be palette swaps of a single drawing
  - identical alpha, identical pixel counts - so keeping four copies of the
  same geometry would have been four times the bytes for no extra art. The
  extraction was verified pixel-for-pixel before the maps were written.

  Only the first column is used: a four-frame flight cycle at 16x15, east
  facing. The perched and standing poses in columns 1 and 2, `branches.png`
  and `Bungie Case.png` are unused.

PROVENANCE
  Supplied by the project owner as `Budgies_Spritesheets.zip`. The archive
  carried no licence file, no readme and no author credit.
